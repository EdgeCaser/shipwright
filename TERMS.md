## No service, no account

Shipwright runs inside the AI tool you install it in, such as Claude Code or Codex. The maintainers don't run a service behind it, don't hold an account for you, and can't see your work. How Shipwright handles data is described in the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

## Your host's terms still apply

The model that does the writing belongs to your host, and that host's terms and usage policies cover what you ask it and what it produces. Websites and APIs the research helper fetches from have their own terms too.

## Check the output

Shipwright produces drafts and recommendations for product work: PRDs, pricing analyses, strategy memos and the like. They can be wrong. A recommendation is only as good as the evidence behind it, and an AI model can misread a source or fill a gap with something plausible. Review anything that matters before you act on it, especially numbers, legal or regulatory claims, and decisions about people.
